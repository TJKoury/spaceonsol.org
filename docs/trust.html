Your web of trust

Sending $SPACE creates a link to a key — that transfer, and the balance sitting at that key, are on-chain and visible to anyone. What the link means is not: your levels, your rules and your revocations are a private mapping held in this browser.

That split is the point. The public part answers one question continuously — have the funds at that key been drained? A key that still holds what you sent it has not been compromised. Your private config decides how much that is worth to you.
